A LITTLE NOTE 🐾

Hey you... 🐱🤍

I honestly don't know how to start a letter like this because there are so many things I want to say.

If I tried sending all of them in one normal message, I'd probably end up writing forever.

So I thought I'd put them here instead.

A little place where I don't have to worry about making the message too long, because let's be honest...

this letter was never going to be short.

You became someone really special to me in a way I didn't expect.

Somewhere between our conversations, random messages, silly moments, busy days and those little windows where we manage to talk...

you became part of my everyday thoughts.

THE BUSY DAYS

I don't need every minute.

I know we both have our own work, responsibilities and things that need our attention.

I don't expect us to disappear from our own lives just to talk all day.

Actually, one of the things I appreciate most is that we can both have our own lives and still make room for each other.

Sometimes it's a long conversation.

Sometimes it's only a few messages.

Sometimes one of us replies later.

But the little effort is still there.

And that means more than constantly being online ever could.

WHAT I APPRECIATE

It's the way we keep finding time.

I appreciate the little things.

The messages.

The jokes.

The random conversations.

The moments when one of us is busy but still checks in.

The way we share little pieces of our days, even when nothing extraordinary happens.

I appreciate that I can tell you random things about my day and somehow make them sound important.

I appreciate your little updates, your excitement, your stories, and even the ordinary moments you decide to share with me.

I hope you know that you don't have to be cheerful every day around me.

You can have tiring days. You can have quiet days. You can have days when you don't feel like doing much.

You don't have to pretend everything is perfect.

I want you to feel comfortable being yourself.

ABOUT US

One day at a time.

I know that distance can make some things difficult.

We cannot simply meet whenever we want, share every little moment in person or turn an ordinary day into a spontaneous adventure together.

But I still believe that meaningful things can grow with patience, honesty, understanding and effort.

I don't expect us to have everything figured out.

I would rather we keep learning, communicating and understanding each other as we go.

When something feels difficult, I hope we can talk about it calmly instead of giving up on understanding one another.

Let's keep choosing kindness, honesty and respect.

A SMALL PROMISE

I'll keep trying.

I'll keep learning how to communicate better, listen more carefully and appreciate the little things.

I won't promise that every day will be perfect.

But I can promise that understanding and respect will always matter to me.
